Open Accounting System Builder is an educational and operational workflow tool.

It does not provide accounting, bookkeeping, tax, legal, audit, assurance, investment, or regulatory filing services. It does not establish that a ledger, policy, financial statement, tax position, filing, or internal control complies with any law or professional standard.

AI-generated classifications, policies, journal entries, reconciliations, and reports are proposals until reviewed and approved by appropriately authorized people. Matters marked `PROFESSIONAL_DECISION_REQUIRED` should be reviewed by a qualified accountant, bookkeeper, tax professional, auditor, lawyer, or other relevant professional in the applicable jurisdiction.

Users are responsible for:

- verifying current laws, standards, tax rules, deadlines, and regulator guidance;
- protecting confidential and restricted information;
- maintaining complete source evidence and backups;
- obtaining necessary authorizations before accessing or writing data;
- reviewing all outputs before posting, closing, filing, paying, or sharing; and
- deciding whether the project is suitable for their circumstances.
