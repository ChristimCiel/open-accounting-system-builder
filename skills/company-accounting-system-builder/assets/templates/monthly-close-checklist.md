# Monthly Close Checklist

- Period:
- System of record:
- Prepared by:
- Reviewed by:
- Status: `OPEN`

## Intake and evidence

- [ ] All in-scope source systems have an as-of timestamp.
- [ ] Transaction inbox has no unexplained unprocessed items.
- [ ] Duplicate, missing-field, company-purpose, and evidence checks were run.

## Reconciliations

- [ ] Bank accounts reconcile to the ledger or have documented differences.
- [ ] Cash and wallets reconcile to the ledger or have documented differences.
- [ ] Credit-card purchases, refunds, statements, payments, and liabilities reconcile.
- [ ] Payment-platform sales, refunds, fees, settlements, and bank receipts reconcile.
- [ ] Accounts receivable and accounts payable subledgers reconcile to the ledger.

## Accounting controls

- [ ] Every posted entry is balanced and traceable to an economic event.
- [ ] Deferred/prepaid/accrued/estimated/foreign-currency items follow confirmed policies.
- [ ] No tax treatment or professional judgment was silently promoted to confirmed status.
- [ ] Open items have owners, next actions, due dates, and close impact.

## Reporting and handoff

- [ ] Cash, accrual, tax-adjustment, and management views are clearly labeled.
- [ ] Significant movements and assumptions are explained.
- [ ] Professional review pack and change log are current.
- [ ] Version manifest was updated and accepted versions were not overwritten.
